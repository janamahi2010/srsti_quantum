Last updated: August 2026

How this site is built

srstiquantum.in is a static website — there's no login system, no payment processing, and no database storing user data. The contact form sends mail directly through your own email client rather than submitting to a server we control, which keeps the attack surface about as small as it gets.

Working with us

If you're evaluating Srsti Quantum as a manufacturing or research partner and need to share device designs, specifications, or other sensitive material, we handle that under NDA and through channels agreed with you directly — not through this website. Ask your point of contact and we'll set that up properly.

Reporting a security issue

Found something wrong — a vulnerability, an exposed file, anything that shouldn't be public? Email security@srstiquantum.in with what you found and how to reproduce it. We'll acknowledge genuine reports and won't pursue legal action against anyone reporting an issue in good faith who doesn't access more data than needed to demonstrate it.
