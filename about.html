Our Company

Precision Quantum Hardware Manufacturing

Srsti Quantum is an advanced quantum hardware manufacturing company dedicated to the fabrication of superconducting quantum processors, microwave resonators, and specialized quantum devices.

We combine expertise in nanofabrication, precision thin-film processing, device characterization, and semiconductor manufacturing to deliver reliable quantum hardware for research institutions, startups, industry, and national laboratories.

Our multidisciplinary team brings together specialists in quantum device engineering, lithography, materials science, microwave engineering, cryogenic measurements, and quality assurance. Working alongside leading academic institutions, research organizations, and technology partners, we develop manufacturing processes that emphasize precision, repeatability, and scalability.

From cleanroom fabrication to comprehensive electrical, microwave, and cryogenic characterization, every device undergoes rigorous validation before delivery. Our goal is to provide world-class quantum hardware manufacturing capabilities that strengthen India's quantum technology ecosystem while serving global research and industrial communities.

Why Indigenous Quantum Hardware Matters

Building quantum hardware domestically isn't just a manufacturing choice - it's a strategic one for India's research and industrial ecosystem.

01

Cheaper

Manufacturing on Indian soil removes import duties, currency conversion losses, and layers of overseas middlemen, lowering acquisition costs for labs and industry.

02

Lesser Lead Times

Local fabrication, packaging, and testing mean devices aren't held up in customs or overseas transit, enabling faster iteration for time-sensitive research programs.

03

No More Dependence on Importing

Every processor and test chip built in-house reduces reliance on foreign suppliers for a strategically critical technology, protecting against external supply shocks.

04

Collaborating with Research Centers & Universities

We work alongside IITs, IISc, and national laboratories to align our fabrication capabilities with real research needs and accelerate technology transfer.

05

Contributing to Indian Economy & Building Specialized Workforce

Every device manufactured here creates skilled jobs in nanofabrication, cryogenic engineering, and device testing, building the workforce India needs to compete globally.
