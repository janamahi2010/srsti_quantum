How we handle information collected through this website.

Last updated: August 2026

What this page covers

This is the privacy policy for srstiquantum.in. It's a simple site — we don't have user accounts, we don't run ads, and we don't track you across the web. Here's what actually happens with your information when you visit.

The contact form

When you fill out the contact form, your browser opens a pre-filled email addressed to hello@srstiquantum.in, and you send it from your own email client. We never see or store the form data on a server — it just arrives in our inbox as a normal email, the same as if you'd written to us directly. We use it to reply to whatever you asked about, and for nothing else.

Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't run any analytics or tracking scripts. If that changes, we'll update this page to say so.

Other links on this site

The contact page embeds a Google Map showing our office location, which means Google may receive your IP address when that map loads. That's the only third-party content on the site.

Questions

Email hello@srstiquantum.in and we'll get back to you.
